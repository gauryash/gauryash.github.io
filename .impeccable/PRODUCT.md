# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The site serves two primary audiences:

- **Hiring managers & recruiters** — reviewing Yash Gaur's background, skills, and experience for quantitative engineering roles. They scan quickly for relevant experience (Python, React, C++, HFT), project depth, and professional polish.
- **Technical peers & the quant/dev community** — reading blog posts, evaluating technical depth, and getting a sense of Yash's thinking and communication style. They value clarity, substance, and authenticity over flash.

## Product Purpose

A personal resume and portfolio site that establishes Yash Gaur's professional identity as a Quantitative Engineer. Success means a visitor can, within 30 seconds, understand who Yash is, what he does, and whether his profile matches their hiring need — while also getting a genuine sense of his technical voice through blog posts and project descriptions.

## Brand Personality

Clean, Professional, Precise.

The tone is confident but not boastful, technical but not jargon-heavy. The site should feel like a well-organized resume brought to life — nothing extra, nothing missing. It communicates competence through clarity of structure, quality of content, and restraint in design.

## Anti-references

- Over-designed "creative agency" portfolios with heavy animations, gradients, and decorative flourishes
- SaaS-style landing pages with big hero sections, CTA buttons, and marketing copy
- Generic "AI-generated" design tells: cream/sand backgrounds, purple-to-blue gradients, cards-within-cards, numbered section markers (01 / 02 / 03), small uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section
- Dated dev portfolio tropes: the green-on-black terminal aesthetic, particle.js backgrounds, bouncing scroll indicators

## Design Principles

1. **Content first, chrome second.** The site's job is to present information clearly. Layout, typography, and spacing serve readability — not decoration.
2. **Precision over personality.** Every design choice should feel intentional and restrained. If it doesn't serve the content, it doesn't belong.
3. **Professional without being boring.** Clean doesn't mean generic. Small moments of craft (type scale, spacing rhythm, subtle transitions) communicate care without shouting.
4. **One page, many contexts.** The homepage works as a standalone resume (scanned quickly) and as a blog platform (read deeply). Both modes should feel equally native.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance target (4.5:1 body text contrast, 3:1 large text)
- Full keyboard navigation support
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — all transitions degrade gracefully
- Dark and light mode both fully tested for contrast
